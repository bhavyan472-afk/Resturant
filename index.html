Our story • Since 2005

A family table, open since 2005.

Kuljit started cooking for neighbours out of a small kitchen with her mother's recipes and a hand-built clay tandoor. Two decades later, the same family still grinds the spices each morning, slow-simmers the daal overnight and greets regulars by name.

Nothing here is rushed. Every curry starts from whole spices, every naan is slapped fresh against the tandoor wall, and every guest is treated like family who just walked into our home.
